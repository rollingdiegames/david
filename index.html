THE DESIGNER

Meet David Wenneker

David Wenneker

Game Designer & Creator

A solo designer who likes rolling dice. My love for rolling dice started in high school playing tabletop role playing games with my friends on weekends. I started designing games in the fall of 2024. I had an idea for a boardgame for about 15 years prior that I did nothing with but it always stuck in my mind for YEARS. One day I told my friends about it. After explaining my idea they said “That sounds cool, go make it any we’ll play it.” and “Hey, have you heard about Protospiel?” A few months later I took my ugly prototype made of battlecaps and index cards and a board scribbled on an offcut piece of luan to protospiel. Thus began my journey into board game design.

I enjoy making games that involve rolling dice and I tend to gravitate towards the fantasy genre in many of my designs, but mainly I make games I think are fun and I want to play.

BEHIND THE GAMES

From first idea to game night.

Every game starts somewhere. Follow the development process as ideas become prototypes, mechanics are tested, and games evolve.

01

Concept

I think up or dream up a game concept that I want to play or I think would be cool. Some are made for the sole purpose of a design contest.

02

Prototype

I begin to assemble bits as placeholders and make paper printouts to be able to test the concept.

03

Playtesting

A working prototype is assembled and playtesting begins. The first few rough plays are where many changes will be made. After a few rounds of testing with needed revisions, if the design is unique and the game functions, it will be refined and made prettier for beta testing.

04

Final Game

The polishing step. The core of the game is completed, final touches needed compiling art, writing a rule book, very few changes will be made at this stage. (Now to figure out what to do with it)
